What SKULOS is

Not a School Management System. An Education Operating System.

Traditional school software solves one problem at a time — attendance here, fees there, results somewhere else. SKULOS instead connects every department, every user, and every workflow into one platform, so information is entered once and used everywhere.

One school owner. One login. Every module they've enabled — nothing they haven't.

What changes on day one

The details schools notice first

✉️

Bulk, everywhere

Import, export, print, promote or message an entire class — or the whole school — in one action instead of one record at a time.

🗂️

One source of truth

A student's record, once created, is the same record the registrar, the class teacher, the bursar and the parent all see.

🎯

Grading, your way

40% CA and 60% exam, or 20/20/60, or something entirely your own — the grading engine has no fixed formula.

🔐

Role-based access

Twenty-one built-in roles, from Bursar to Hostel Master, each with permissions your admin can adjust without a developer.

💳

Fees, collected flexibly

Paystack, bank transfer with proof of payment, or the gateway your school already uses — installments included.

🧾

Nothing changes quietly

Every edit is logged — who, when, from where, and what it was before — so records stay accountable.

🔑

One login, everywhere

A single identity carries a user through every module they're permitted to see — no separate passwords per department.

🤖

Built-in AI, not bolted on

Report card comments get a first draft automatically, and at-risk students surface before the term ends, not after.

🎁

A free term to prove it

Every new school starts with one full academic term at no cost, with no card required to begin.
