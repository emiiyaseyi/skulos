Questions, answered

The things schools ask us most. If your question is not here, reach out and we will help directly.

SKULOS is a complete education operating system. Instead of separate tools for records, results, fees and communication, it brings admissions, academics, finance, communication and learning into one platform, with one login and one source of truth for your school.

Pricing is per active student, per academic term. Starter is 500 naira, Growth is 800 naira, Professional is 1,200 naira, and Enterprise is 1,500 naira, each per student per term. You can pay termly or annually, and annual billing is three times the termly rate.

Every school gets its own dedicated database, so no school can ever see another school's data. Access is permission based, every change is logged, and data is encrypted. Our handling aligns with the NDPR in Nigeria and the GDPR in Europe. See the Privacy Policy for full detail.

SKULOS launches as a web application that works on any modern browser, on desktop, tablet or phone. The architecture is API first, so parent, teacher and student mobile apps can follow without a redesign.

Every school gets a free subdomain at launch, for example yourschool.skulosapp.com, with support for a custom domain you already own once you are ready.

A guided setup wizard walks you through sections, classes, grading, houses, and staff and student import. Most schools are operational in about fifteen minutes.
