Questions

Pricing, explained plainly

How does per student pricing work?

Each plan is priced per active student, per academic term. Your termly bill is your student count multiplied by your plan's rate, it adjusts naturally as enrollment changes.

What happens after my free year?

Every new school gets one full year at no cost. Before it ends, you choose a plan to continue, nothing is billed automatically without your confirmation.

Termly or annual, what's the difference?

Termly billing charges once per academic term. Annual billing covers three terms upfront at 3× the termly rate, paid once a year instead of three times.

How do I pay?

Through Paystack, or by bank transfer with proof of payment uploaded for confirmation, whichever your school prefers.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. As your school's needs change, you can move to a different plan, and additional modules or storage can be added independently of your base plan.

What if I'm not sure which plan fits?

Reach out and describe your school, student count, the modules you actually need, and we'll help you pick the right starting point.
